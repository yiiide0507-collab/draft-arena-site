Privacy Policy for Draft Arena

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

Draft Arena does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking. The game makes no network requests while you play.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • your settings (difficulty, arena, speed, sound, music, vibration, graphics and accessibility choices);
  • your game progress: the current run checkpoint, best results per difficulty, experience level, achievements, unlocked cosmetics, discovered characters and a short run history.

This data never leaves your device through the app. It is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup service, Android may include this game progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the developer of Draft Arena does not receive or have access to it.

Permissions

  • Internet — required by Android's WebView to display the game, which is bundled inside the app. The game itself blocks all network connections.
  • Vibrate — used only for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal information.

Third parties

Draft Arena contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any data collection, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the change will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the Draft Arena site repository.
